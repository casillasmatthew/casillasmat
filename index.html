About Me

Hi, I'm Matthew Casillas. I am not particularly good at art, but I do like to try new things. And so through this online portfolio I will showcase and document my projects from my Art 10c class.

Featured Work

Project 1

In this project I scanned a quarter, dime, nickel, and a penny to then change the images on the original coins into something/someone more progressive in order to match my ideologies.

Project 2

For this project I 3D modeled a little cat plush keychain I own. After creating the model in blender I added image textures in order to make it look robotic.

Project 3

For this project I created a video showcasing commen sights in my everyday life. But with added text I made it so it's a personal little monologue, that lets the viewer peep into what goes on what I'm always thinking about. With a view basic transitions and text affects, plus copyright free music I wanted to make this little slice of life video as authentic to me as possible.
